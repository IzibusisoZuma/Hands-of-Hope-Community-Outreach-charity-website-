Our story, mission & the people behind it

Our History

From a folding table to a registered NPO

Hands of Hope Community Outreach began in 2015 when five friends started cooking soup for the men and women sleeping rough near Braamfontein station. What started as a folding table and two pots is now a registered non-profit organisation serving over 300 meals a week.

Over the following years, the need we saw kept widening: people who wanted work but lacked basic skills, and people who had nowhere safe to sleep. So the soup kitchen grew two siblings — a youth skills-training programme and a shelter-referral service — each run by people who once relied on Hands of Hope themselves.

Mission & Vision

Why we do this work

Mission

To restore dignity to vulnerable individuals by providing food security, shelter referrals, and pathways to self-sufficiency.

Vision

A Johannesburg where no one goes hungry or without a place to call home.

Governance

Accountable, transparent, community-led

Hands of Hope is governed by a five-person voluntary board that meets quarterly, with an independent annual financial review shared with all corporate sponsors and major donors.

Who we serve

Our community

  • Prospective volunteers looking for meaningful, regular involvement
  • Residents of inner-city Johannesburg who rely on our meals, training, or shelter referrals
