Three programmes. One community.

Programme 01

Community Soup Kitchen

Every weekday from 11:30 to 13:30, our Braamfontein kitchen serves a hot, nutritious meal to anyone who needs one. No referral, ID, or means test is required — just a bowl and a seat at the table.

  • Over 300 meals served weekly across two serving points
  • Menus planned by a volunteer nutritionist for balanced meals
  • Ingredients sourced from local wholesalers and surplus-food partners

Volunteer shifts

We need help with prep (09:00-11:30), serving (11:30-13:30), and clean-up (13:30-14:30), Monday to Friday.

Programme 02

Youth Skills Training

Unemployed young people aged 18-28 can join our 8-week training cohorts covering hospitality basics, entry-level computer literacy, and small-trade skills such as basic carpentry and sewing.

  • Free training, tools, and a certificate of completion
  • Job-placement support with our network of local employers
  • Over 60 graduates placed in employment since 2019
Programme 03

Shelter Referrals

Our case workers maintain relationships with a network of partner shelters across Johannesburg, helping individuals and families find a safe, verified bed for the night — especially during the winter months.

  • Same-day referrals during the Johannesburg winter shelter season
  • Follow-up support to help people access ID documents and grants
  • Priority support for women, children, and people with disabilities

Need a referral, or know someone who does?

Our enquiry form includes a direct option for shelter referral requests, reviewed daily by our case workers.
